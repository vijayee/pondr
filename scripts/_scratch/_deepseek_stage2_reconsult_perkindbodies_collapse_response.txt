2. COORDINATION MECHANISMS FOR PER-KIND BODIES: ALL DEAD

Given the root cause, none of these can escape the incompatible-subspace collapse. They are ranked and refuted:

- (a) Warm-start from the same shared #5 body: The bodies will still diverge to specialize to kind-specific features, and the shared head will again face misaligned representations. The collapse will recur as soon as the bodies drift.
- (b) Freeze bodies → train head → unfreeze: The head will learn a mapping for the initial aligned bodies, but once unfrozen, the bodies will drift and the head will break. Staged training does not create a common subspace; it only delays the collapse.
- (c) Alignment/orthogonality regularizer: Forcing bodies to stay aligned defeats the purpose of per-kind decomposition (they become a shared body). An orthogonality regularizer would push them apart, making the head’s job harder. Neither solves the absence of a common cross-kind subspace in h.

Per-kind decomposition is dead for this frozen backbone. Do not invest further.
