**3. Structural insight**

Yes. In a single coherent thread, “recall the topically‑relevant prior turns” is the wrong unit. The right unit is **“recall the specific turn this anaphoric query points back to”** – a pointer‑like, coreference resolution task. The current salience gate tries to solve it with a topical‑relevance scorer, which is why it drowns in same‑thread competitors.

This does **not** demand an immediate architecture rewrite. A cross‑encoder reranker approximates a pointer by scoring deep semantic relatedness, and for a budget of 3 it is sufficient. If the project later moves to a dedicated coreference head, that head would still benefit from the same cross‑encoder signal. For now, the pointer insight simply reinforces that **ranking must be driven by conceptual relatedness, not lexical overlap**.
